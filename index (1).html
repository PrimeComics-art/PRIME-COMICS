Prime Universe

One shared world where superheroes, advanced technology, alien biology and myths from countries around the globe all affect each other.

  1. Earth runs out of timeA cosmic disaster threatens Earth’s habitability, and its rotation slows, stops or reverses.
  2. Humanity leavesGeneration ships, built from space waste and resources gathered worldwide, carry the survivors away.
  3. A new planetA world about 12 times the size of Earth, with four suns, a 24-hour day and a 36-hour night.
  4. A new societyLiving contracts and a new dollar system shape daily life.
  5. Powers emergeA travel accident and the new system change some humans into heroes, while others already walk the planet.
